The distribution

Every trade pays a 1% fee. The USDC half of that fee splits between holders of record and the deployer. The token half is burned.

So the supply shrinks and holders are paid in dollars, by people trading. That is the whole mechanism. There is nothing else under it.

No amount is promised. No amount is projected. Distributions rise and fall with volume, and volume can go to zero.

The rules

  • No presale, and no allocation handed to anyone.
  • The deployer bought 4% in the launch transaction, said so a day early, published the wallet, and cannot sell before 16 September 2027.
  • Liquidity is locked in a contract with no withdraw path, for anyone, including us.
  • The contract has no owner and cannot mint. Nobody can change it, including us.
  • There is no roadmap. There is a date.
