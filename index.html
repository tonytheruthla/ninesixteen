Doc. 916-003 · The mechanism

Every trade pays holders.
In actual dollars.

A 1% fee on every trade. The dollar half is split between holders of record and the deployer wallet. The token half is destroyed forever. Supply falls, holders get paid, nobody has to be trusted.

To holders Fee to deployer Burned

The deployer earns a share of trading fees and holds no tokens at all. No amount is promised and no amount is projected. Distributions rise and fall with trading, and trading can go to zero.
