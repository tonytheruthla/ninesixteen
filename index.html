Doc. 916-003 · The mechanism

Every trade pays you.
In actual dollars.

A 1% fee on every trade. The dollar half splits between holders of record and the deployer. The token half is destroyed forever. Supply falls, holders get paid, nobody has to be trusted.

To holders To deployer Burned

No amount is promised and no amount is projected. Distributions rise and fall with trading, and trading can go to zero.
